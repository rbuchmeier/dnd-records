Player letterFrom Mara

To the others,

I thought the bell would wake the whole keep. Instead, it rang once beneath our feet, and every lock in the passage answered. We stood still long enough to hear a second sound: water moving behind the stone.

I have kept the scrap of map we found in the arch. The mark on it matches the one on my old compass. Next week, I think we should follow the water before anyone above us notices the door is open.

Mara

Player letterFrom Iven

Dear Tamsin,

The bridge held, although none of us believed it would. On the far bank we found the watchman's cold camp and a fresh path cut through the reeds. Someone reached the bell tower before us.

I left a stone at the crossing so we can find it again in the fog. If you come after us, follow the north bank until you see three white posts. We will wait one more night by the tower.

Iven
