Tovin, I thought courage would feel like certainty. Tonight it felt like taking one more step after everyone had stopped pretending the road was safe.

Letter I · Fen to Captain Vale · sealed

Captain, Greywater is empty. We found no bodies, but the chapel register ends on the same date as the king’s journey. Someone has removed every name written after sundown.
