09 October 2026Session 18 / Week 18

The Bell Under Blackwater

Chronicle · full panels

Player letter · complete

From Mara to her brother09 October

Dear Tovin,

We found the road you insisted was only a story. It rises out of the reeds when the water draws back, straight as a ruler and pale as bone. None of us spoke until we reached the bell.

Its rope had rotted away, but the iron was warm. On the door behind it I found Ferryman Hale’s name, cut there by someone who knew his hand. I think the river kept more than his boat, and I am afraid we have just asked it to give something back.

I will write again when we know what answered.
Mara

02 October 2026Session 17 / Week 17

At the Reed Crossing

Chronicle · full panels

Player letter · complete

From Ivo to the harbor master02 October

Harbor Master,

Your ferryman did not meet us at the crossing. His pole lay on the near bank, and there were prints leading across the bridge, but none returning. We waited until dusk and heard a bell from somewhere below the water.

At dawn I drew the banks as they stood. The new line on my chart is no error: a road lies beneath the reeds and points toward the old causeway. We mean to follow it when the tide falls next week.

Keep the east lantern lit for us.
Ivo

Design rationale
