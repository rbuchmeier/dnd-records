## The chamber

Below the keep, the party found a small chamber with four alcoves. Three held weathered names. The fourth had been left blank.

At its center rested a gold circlet, half buried in roots. Elara lifted it and heard the bell once more, impossibly distant. The company agreed to carry the crown until they understood who had left it there.
