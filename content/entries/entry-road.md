The company reached the last mile stone at dusk. Their guide's lantern crossed the ruined arch, though no one held it, and settled in a pool of pale light on the other side.

Elara found a crown carved into the stone. One point had been scratched away. Beneath it, Rowan discovered fresh boot prints leading toward the keep.
