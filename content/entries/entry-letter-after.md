Dear Mara,

The bell rang when I said my name. I know how that sounds on paper. It sounded stranger in the tower.

We found a crown below the keep. It is small, gold, and cold even near a fire. There is a place waiting for a fourth name beside it. I do not intend to give it mine.

If this letter reaches you before I do, please know that I am with friends who remember how to laugh, even here.

With love,\
Elara
