## Weather and ground

Fine rain since morning. The old road is wet enough to hold a clear print. Four sets of boots entered the arch before ours; only three sets appear to have come back.

No birdsong after dusk. Found a tuft of gray wool caught on a thorn at shoulder height. Wrapped it in paper for later comparison.
