Dear Mara,

I promised to write before we passed the thirteenth mile stone. I had imagined a crossroads, an inn, perhaps a sign with the paint worn away. Instead there is an arch standing alone in the rain. Our lantern has gone ahead of us.

Rowan says the road beyond it has seen traffic this week. I hope he is wrong, though he rarely is about tracks.

Keep the blue ribbon for me. I will want it when we come home.

Your sister,\
Elara
