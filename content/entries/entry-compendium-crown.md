## Description

A narrow gold circlet with four open points. Its inner edge bears no royal seal or maker's mark. Despite its name, it is solid metal and surprisingly light.

The crown rested in a chamber with four alcoves. Three were inscribed; one was blank. The bell sounded once when Elara lifted the circlet.
